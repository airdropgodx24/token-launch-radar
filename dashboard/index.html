Token Launch Radar

Every protocol that records no token yet, ranked by what its launch could be worth, with a farm plan for each one.

The model. Implied value = TVL × category multiple × momentum × seasoning. The multiple prices what a launch captures for that kind of protocol (DEXs 1.60, derivatives 1.50, bridges 0.70, default 0.90). Momentum reads the 7-day TVL change, clamped to −50%..+100% and mapped onto 0.75..1.50 through a square-root response, so a freak week saturates instead of ruling the board. Seasoning rises from 0.75 to 1.25 over four years of running with no token. No name is ever scored by hand.
